### Method

The local archive loader keeps the longer title-and-body version of each record of the same type and ID. Selection requires a match to the report's listed arthritis-community names plus wording about hands or related activities. Topic expressions run against title and body together; accounts are deduplicated within each topic, excluding blank and deleted labels. Categories can overlap. The companion report supplies the filters, evidence map, and reproduction sequence.

### Limits

This is a convenience archive, not a survey of U.S. adults. Country, adult status, diagnosis, product ownership, and treatment outcomes are unverified. A mention may describe a question, someone else's experience, or a negation. No raw posts, usernames, or quotations are distributed. The intended reader is an adult in the United States with hand arthritis; that audience does not describe a verified population sample.
